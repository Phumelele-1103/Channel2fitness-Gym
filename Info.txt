In 2022, the gym received a R60 000 grant from the National Youth Development Agency (NYDA), which helped Mahlalela buy equipment.
This helped him attract more clients, which ultimately led to the creation of four permanent jobs for residents.

He was also able to buy more equipment through the Asset Assist Programme of the Small Enterprise Development and Finance Agency (SEFDA),
which further increased membership to approximately 100 individuals.

In 2025, the gym won first place at the Mpumalanga Bambelela Business Awards. This validated Mahlalela’s efforts in pursuing social 
entrepreneurship and developing his community.
He encouraged young people to chase their dreams and work while they are still young.

“Government has various opportunities and initiatives that support young business owners. Register your business and apply for those opportunities,
and you will realise that it is a privilege to be a young person living in South Africa today,” he concluded.
